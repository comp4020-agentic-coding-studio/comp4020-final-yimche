# Crit 10 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

This week the breakthrough I think was in the riff-10 pod meeting, coming up
with more features, as well as demonstrating and live testing with multiple
people in the lab.
This allowed me to hear suggestions, feedback and ideas that I myself would not
be able to come up with myself.


## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

It made me want to put things in front of people sooner. Up to this point I
had been testing Torchlight mostly by myself and with the agent, and a game
about finding other people can't really be judged that way. Watching others
play it in the lab showed me what was confusing and what was fun much faster
than any test could, and the ideas from the pod gave me most of what I built
in the weeks after. The agent can write a feature quickly, but it can't tell
me which feature is worth writing. I want to be the developer who goes and
asks, and then brings what I hear back as rules and tests the agent works
under.
