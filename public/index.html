    Torchlight

    Everyone seeks. Hold your torch on someone's back or side to catch them, and don't let them do it to you.

    Moving

    Walk with WASD or the arrow keys, or drag on the maze on a phone. Your torch points at the mouse, whichever way you walk; on a phone it points where you walk.

    Catching

    You see only what your torch lights and a small circle around you. Keep your beam on someone from behind or from the side and they're out. The quarter in front of them is safe: they can see you coming.

    Bump into someone head on, or light each other at once, and you're both stunned: no catches between you until the light comes off and the ring stops flashing.

    The flash

    Every ten seconds the whole maze lights up for a moment, and everyone still in shows with it.

    Powerups

    Walk over one to carry it, one at a time, and use it with Space or by tapping the slot at the bottom.

    • Speed runs you faster for a few seconds.
    • X-ray lets your torch see through walls, though it only catches in the open.
    • Teleport drops you a few rooms ahead of where you face.
    • Freeze stops everyone in your beam for five seconds.
    • Decoy leaves a still copy of you for eight seconds. It looks just like you to everyone else, and can't be caught.
    • Blackout shortens every rival's torch to half its reach for four seconds.

    The falling floor

    A minute in, the floor starts falling away from the edges toward the middle. A tile shakes and glows red before it goes; step onto a fallen one and you're out.
